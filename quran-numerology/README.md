# The Mu'awwidhatayn: a numerical and structural study

A study of Surah Al-Falaq (113) and Surah An-Nas (114): their numbers, letters, structure and protective function.

| Part | File | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01-surah-al-falaq.md](01-surah-al-falaq.md) | Al-Falaq's position, words, letters, abjad values and root echoes |
| 2 | [02-al-falaq-counter-pattern.md](02-al-falaq-counter-pattern.md) | Al-Falaq as a counter-pattern to sorcery: Quran and hadith, classical scholars, ancient binding magic, behavioural science |
| 3 | [03-an-nas-and-the-pair.md](03-an-nas-and-the-pair.md) | An-Nas, and the two surahs as one two-layer defence (outer and inner) |
| 4 | [04-shams-al-maarif-and-the-muawwidhatayn.md](04-shams-al-maarif-and-the-muawwidhatayn.md) | Comparison with *Shams al-Ma'arif*: the book's letter-science lens on the surahs, and where the two part ways |

Every claim is tagged **[Fact]**, **[Tradition]**, **[Reflection]** or, in Part 4, **[Lettrist]**. Each part ends
with a ranking of how strong its claims are.

## Reproducing the numbers

```
curl -sSLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mustafa0x/quran-morphology/master/quran-morphology.txt
python3 abjad_analysis.py quran-morphology.txt 113
python3 abjad_analysis.py quran-morphology.txt 114
```
